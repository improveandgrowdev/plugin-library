Claude skills

Skills our team has built for Claude. They sync to everyone in the Improve & Grow Claude organization automatically, so there's nothing to install. Type the /command in Claude, or just ask for the task and Claude picks the skill up.

Maintained by the Improve & Grow dev team. To add a plugin or skill, edit the PLUGINS or SKILLS list in index.html in the plugin-library repo.
